A little about me

Most recently, I was a Senior Product Designer at Retool and GitHub. I focused on making powerful software intuitive and human, without lowering the ceiling for what people can do. Some of my work was open source*.
I've also collaborated on new consumer products, and I teach interaction design at Parsons School of Design.
Before all of this, I spent some time at Upstatement and Ronik, led the crew at Scout, and graduated from Northeastern†.
I grew up in the ‘burbs of New York‡, make a lot of self deprecating jokes§, and I'm notorious for being average at a thousand hobbies—most recently piano bass, mini Monday Tuesday crossword puzzles, knitting painting, and journaling writing.¶
